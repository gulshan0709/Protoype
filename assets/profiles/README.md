`demo-man.png` and `demo-woman.png` are high-resolution fictional portraits created with the built-in imagegen tool. The shared `demoPortrait` resolver replaces only the original bundled dummy photos in directories, forms and face captures, including previously stored demo records. Uploaded photos retain their own URI. All assets work offline.

Generation prompts: photorealistic professional identity headshots of fictional Indian adults in their early 30s; front-facing, natural friendly expressions, soft even daylight, pale gray studio background, crisp natural skin texture, square composition, no text or watermark. Man: neat dark hair and navy collared shirt. Woman: shoulder-length dark hair and light blue blouse.

The four original JPEGs and embedded record images came from [Random User](https://randomuser.me/photos); retained as legacy demo identifiers.
